About Me

My Journey Into Software Testing

From Mechanical Engineering and Project Management to Professional Scuba Diving Instructor and finally into Software Quality Assurance.

Professional Background

My career started in mechanical engineering, metrology and quality control where precision, documentation and analytical thinking were essential.

Later I worked as a Project Manager, improving communication, planning, documentation and problem-solving skills.

During the COVID pandemic I relocated to Bali, became a Professional Scuba Diving Instructor and eventually a Dive Center Manager.

Why QA?

Software testing combines everything I enjoy:

  • Analytical thinking
  • Attention to detail
  • Problem solving
  • Documentation
  • Continuous improvement
  • Learning new technologies

I enjoy finding issues before users do and helping deliver high-quality software.
